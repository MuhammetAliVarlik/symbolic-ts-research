# White-noise sanity check — is F0-07's similarity real, or a sigma-binning artifact?

Generated by `scripts/run_white_noise_sanity_check.py`. Run before F0-10, per instruction:
F0-10's transfer result is much easier to interpret correctly once it's known whether the
binning method itself manufactures apparent cross-domain structure.

## Method

Synthetic white noise (iid Gaussian, zero real temporal structure), injected directly as
the `change` channel (skipping the level→diff step, since the point is *no* real
structure at all), matched **per series** to each real domain's own observation count and
change-variance, then run through the exact same `volatility` → sigma-binning →
`tokenize` → transition-matrix → Frobenius/KL-vs-permutation-null pipeline as F0-06/F0-07.
Compared against F0-07's real, granularity-matched result (finance vs. `ett_daily`):
Frobenius 1.0647, symmetrised KL 0.3605 bits.

**Side check run first:** does the raw variance chosen for the synthetic noise matter at
all? Generated finance-sample-sized noise at (a) its matched real variance and (b) unit
variance, and compared their token distributions: Jensen-Shannon divergence = 0.00195
(bounded [0,1], i.e. essentially zero). Confirms directly, not just by argument, that
sigma-binning normalises away the raw scale of its input by construction — the only
matching criterion that actually matters for this pipeline's output is **sample size**,
not variance. "Matched to variance" and "matched to sample size" are not two independent
knobs for this specific binning design.

**A single noise draw was checked for stability before trusting it for anything.**
15 independent noise-vs-noise realisations gave Frobenius norm std = 0.1493 (range
0.72–1.35) — comparable in size to the effect being measured. A single draw is not a
reliable basis for a conclusion this consequential, so every noise-involving comparison
here is run **10 independent times** and reported as a distribution, with one full
permutation-null pipeline run on top for direct comparability with F0-07's own reporting
format.

## Result

Real value (Frobenius 1.0647, KL 0.3605) against each comparison's 10-replication
distribution:

| comparison | statistic | replication mean (sd) | real value's percentile in this distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| noise vs noise | Frobenius | 0.9531 (0.2516) | 80th |
| noise vs noise | symmetrised KL | 0.2349 (0.0203) | 100th |
| noise(finance-matched) vs **real** ett_daily | Frobenius | 1.4344 (0.0442) | **0th** |
| noise(finance-matched) vs **real** ett_daily | symmetrised KL | 0.3612 (0.0058) | **40th** |
| noise(ett_daily-matched) vs **real** finance | Frobenius | 1.2557 (0.0676) | **0th** |
| noise(ett_daily-matched) vs **real** finance | symmetrised KL | 0.3906 (0.0293) | 20th |

## Interpretation — the two statistics disagree, and that disagreement is the finding

**Frobenius norm supports genuine structure, cleanly.** The real finance-vs-ett_daily
value sits at the 0th percentile of *both* noise-vs-real distributions — smaller (more
similar) than every one of 10 independent noise replications matched to either domain's
sample profile. Real domains resemble each other, by this statistic, distinctly more than
noise resembles either of them. It sits at the 80th percentile of noise-vs-noise's own
(very wide) distribution — not clearly distinguishable from pure chance-vs-chance there,
but that's a far weaker concern than "real looks like noise-vs-real," which is what would
have confirmed the artifact hypothesis outright.

**Symmetrised KL is genuinely ambiguous, and in one direction specifically concerning.**
Real finance-vs-ett_daily's KL (0.3605) sits almost exactly in the *middle* of what
finance-shaped noise achieves against real ett_daily (40th percentile of a tight
distribution, mean 0.3612 — a 0.0007-bit difference). **By this statistic, real finance
contributes nothing distinguishable from a structureless process with finance's sample
size and variance, when compared against ett_daily.** The reverse direction is somewhat
more reassuring (ett_daily-shaped noise vs. real finance sits at the 20th percentile,
i.e. real ett_daily looks a bit more finance-like than noise shaped like it does), but not
as decisively as the Frobenius result.

**Neither of the two pre-specified outcomes materialised.** This is not "(a) and (b) look
comparable to the real result" (Frobenius clearly rules that out), and it is not "(a) and
(b) are clearly weaker across the board" either (KL doesn't clearly rule out the artifact
explanation in the finance→ett_daily direction). The two headline statistics from F0-07
disagree with each other under this check, in a way neither did in F0-07 itself (there,
Frobenius and KL agreed at 96.8th/80.4th percentile, same direction, same conclusion).

## What this means for F0-10

**Weight F0-07's Frobenius-based similarity finding with more confidence than its
KL-based one.** The Frobenius result survives this sanity check; the KL result, in the
finance→ett_daily direction specifically, does not clearly survive it. F0-10's Markov
cross-domain-vs-in-domain comparison should be read with this asymmetry in mind — if
F0-10's own result leans on a KL-like or likelihood-based comparison, the same caution
applies there. This does not by itself demand reconsidering D1 (the side check confirms
sigma-binning behaves as intended — scale-invariant, not artificially inflating
similarity through variance-matching), but it does mean **F0-07's transfer-supporting
evidence should now be described as "one of two statistics survives an artifact check,"
not "the domains are measurably similar," until F0-10 adds a third, independent line of
evidence.**
